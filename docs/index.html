SwipeWise

Learn foreign words with flashcards: flip a card, then swipe right if you knew it, left if you didn't.

Download for Android

Features

  • Courses – one per language you're learning, each with its own word lists.
  • Word lists – e.g. one per lesson; add words quickly, merge lists, search.
  • Flexible sessions – all words or selected lists, words added recently, not revised for a while, or the ones you didn't remember; a new random order every time.
  • Pronunciation – hear every word, read by your phone in the language you're learning.
  • Google Sheets – optionally keep a course's words in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive and edit them on your computer; the app syncs both ways.
  • Works offline – no account needed.

Privacy

Your words stay on your phone and, if you connect Google Sheets, in your own Google Drive. The app can only access the spreadsheets it creates, and the developer never receives your data. Privacy policy
