Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

SwipeWise is a flashcard app for learning foreign words. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it is stored. In short: your words stay on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives any of your data.

Data stored on your phone

Your courses, word lists, words, example sentences, practice results (when a word was last revised and whether you remembered it) and recent sessions are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Google account and Google Sheets (optional)

You can connect a course to Google Sheets. Then you sign in with your Google account and the app:

  • asks only for the permission drive.file – access to files the app itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive;
  • creates one spreadsheet per course in your Google Drive and keeps your words in it, in sync with the app;
  • uses your Google account's email address only to show which account is connected, on your device.

The data is sent directly between your phone and Google's servers over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. The spreadsheets belong to you; you can open, edit, share or delete them in Google Drive at any time.

SwipeWise's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Pronunciation

Words are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google's or Samsung's), as set up in your phone's settings. That engine is part of your phone, not of SwipeWise.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • No account with the developer and no developer servers – nothing is collected, sold or shared.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your phone.
  • Delete the "SwipeWise – …" spreadsheets in your Google Drive to delete the synced copies.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
